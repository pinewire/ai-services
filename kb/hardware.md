# Laptop battery drains quickly

A laptop that loses charge unusually fast may have a background endpoint scan
or a degraded battery. Ask the customer to connect the charger, restart once,
and check the battery health report. If health is below 80 percent or the
laptop shuts down unexpectedly, open a hardware replacement request.

# Broken laptop screen

For a cracked or flickering screen, ask the customer to connect an external
monitor if available and attach a photo to the hardware ticket. Do not ask the
customer to open the device. Hardware support schedules a replacement panel
or loaner after confirming the asset tag.
